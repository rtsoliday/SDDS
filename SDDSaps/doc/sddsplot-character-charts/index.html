sddsplot character charts

32 named fonts and the original Greek and special-symbol sets. Click a chart to open its full-resolution PNG.

Codes are title/label text, not complete shell commands. Preserve dollar signs; escape commas and quotes as required by the sddsplot option parser and your calling language. A space renders blank. Literal tilde and backslash are unsupported. Glyphs are fitted individually for legibility, not shown at their relative typographic sizes.

For inline switches, $r restores your configured default font; $e ends the original special-symbol mode. Amber dots mark legacy glyph mappings, which can override the selected font. Charts assume normal text mode on entry.
